Double Majors in Ethnic Studies

The 30-credit Ethnic Studies major can be paired with a second major in the social sciences, humanities, public health, social work, or STEM. The program’s interdisciplinary, community-oriented training emphasizes collaboration and cross-cultural understanding. Courses required for declared double majors are generally considered eligible for financial aid.
