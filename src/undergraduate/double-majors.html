Double Majors

Pair an Ethnic Studies Major with another degree in social sciences, the humanities, public health, social work, or STEM. Double majoring provides a distinct advantage. Ethnic Studies is a 30-credit Major that aligns smoothly with the credit requirements of another Major. Courses required for declared double majors are generally considered eligible for financial aid. The interdisciplinary, community-oriented training in Ethnic Studies equips students to lead in settings where collaboration and cross-cultural understanding are essential.
