Combined Bachelor’s and Master’s Pathways in Ethnic Studies (BAM)

The combined bachelor’s and master’s (BAM) pathways connect undergraduate study in ethnic studies with graduate study in education or public administration. By double-counting eligible coursework toward both degrees and taking approved graduate courses at undergraduate tuition rates, students can reduce the time and cost of completing their degrees. Depending on the pathway, students may complete both degrees in about five years.

Available Pathways

  1. BA in Ethnic Studies and MEd in Educational Administration. Well suited for students interested in entry and mid-level administrative positions in higher education (e.g., student affairs, student services, housing, financial aid, admissions).
  2. BA in Ethnic Studies and MEd in Educational Foundations. Well suited for students interested in becoming an educator or research scholar intent on positively impacting education policy and practice.
  3. BA in Ethnic Studies and Master of Public Administration (MPA). Well suited for students interested in leadership positions across government, nonprofit, and community organizations, equipping them to make an impact in Hawaiʻi, the U.S., and the Asia-Pacific region.

BAM Pathway Advantages

  • Save money: Approved graduate coursework taken at undergraduate tuition rates can reduce the cost of completing both degrees.
  • Save time: Double-counting eligible courses toward both degrees can shorten the combined course of study.
  • Gain a competitive advantage: Graduate study develops additional knowledge and skills for professional and academic goals.

BAM Pathway Requirements

Students must apply at the beginning of their junior year or in the second-to-last year before completion of their undergraduate studies. Application procedures and deadlines should be confirmed with the graduate program offering the selected pathway.

All applicants must:

  1. Have declared ethnic studies as an undergraduate major.
  2. Have a minimum GPA of 3.0.
  3. Have successfully completed ES 480 or ES 486 or ES 493 with a grade of B or better.
