Department of Ethnic Studies

What world do you want to make? Ethnic Studies provides a home for students eager to learn the skills required to create meaningful change toward a more just future for our communities. Our globally recognized faculty draw on their extensive expertise and experience in facilitating brave spaces to learn together and respond to the critical challenges of the moment. We encourage students to approach identities and institutions through interdisciplinary and transformative frameworks. We empower you to apply academic knowledge beyond the classroom, cultivating practical and creative leadership skills to change our world for the better!
