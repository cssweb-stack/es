ES: What World Do You Want to Make?

E komo mai — Welcome to the Department of Ethnic Studies within the College of Social Sciences at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. Students find a home for developing the knowledge and skills to create meaningful change toward a more just future for their communities. Globally recognized faculty bring extensive expertise and experience to shared learning and critical questions about identities and institutions. Through interdisciplinary study and community engagement, students apply academic knowledge beyond the classroom and develop practical, creative leadership skills.
