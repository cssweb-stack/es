Ethnic Studies Faculty

Ethnic Studies faculty bring expertise in local, national, and global political and economic trends, with a focus on the intersections of race, class, ethnicity, gender, and indigeneity. Their research, teaching, and community engagement address questions of power, identity, social justice, and decolonial practices. Through advising and mentoring, faculty help students develop as critical thinkers and compassionate community leaders prepared to address complex challenges.
