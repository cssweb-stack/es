Island Connections

Island Connections is a monthly television program produced by the UH Mānoa Department of Ethnic Studies that deals with local and global issues and their impact on Hawaii's people. Hosted by Ethnic Studies Professor Ibrahim Aoude, the call-in show airs live on the second Friday of each month at 7 pm on the following channels:

  • Hawaiʻi (Big Island)  - Oceanic Cable Channel 355, QAM 46.55
  • Kauai  - Oceanic Cable Channel 355, QAM 46.55
  • Maui  - Oceanic Cable Channel 355, QAM 27.55
  • Oʻahu  - Oceanic Cable Channel 355, QAM 46.55

Island Connections is rebroadcasted on these channels on the fourth and/or fifth Friday of the month at 7 pm.
