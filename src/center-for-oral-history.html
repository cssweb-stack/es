Center for Oral History

The Center for Oral History in the Department of Ethnic Studies was first established in 1976 by the Hawaiʻi State Legislature as a part of the Social Science Research Institute in the College of Social Sciences at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.

The center collects, documents, preserves, and highlights the recollections of Native Hawaiians and the multiethnic people of Hawaiʻi. Its oral histories and interpretive materials document lifeways, historical events, social movements, and Hawaiʻi’s role in a globalizing world. Books, articles, podcasts, and videos make these histories available for broad use. The center also serves as a resource for oral history materials and trains groups and individuals in oral history research.
