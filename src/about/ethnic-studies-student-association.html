Ethnic Studies Student Association (ESSA)

ESSA is a nonprofit organization based at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. Its central goal is to create an open dialogue about culture, identity, and sociopolitical issues explored in ethnic studies courses and in life. The association takes a critical stance against racism, sexism, homophobia, classism, and the systematic marginalization of people of color.

ESSA has worked with artists across disciplines to connect students and community members through creative expression. Past concerts and events have featured politically conscious artists whose work centers on identity formation, culture, and sociopolitical issues. The association seeks to provide a safe and supportive atmosphere where artists can showcase their talent, find their voice, and inspire others.
