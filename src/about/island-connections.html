Island Connections

Island Connections is a television program produced by the Department of Ethnic Studies at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa that addresses local and global issues and their impact on Hawaiʻi’s people.

Visit the UH Online Friday cable programming schedule for broadcast times and UH Cable viewing information for channels and service providers.
